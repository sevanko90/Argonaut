# CSV and TSV

Comma- and tab-separated files open as a grid of columns, however many rows they have.

- Columns are sized to fit their contents; drag to resize them, and pan across wide tables.
- Scroll from the first row to the last at once - rows are read only as they come on screen.
- Search finds text anywhere in the file.

To look at a slice of a huge export, open a range of its lines from the
[command line](../guide/command-line.md): `Argonaut export.csv --lines 500k+1000`. Or switch to the
[text view](text.md) from the status bar to see the raw rows.
